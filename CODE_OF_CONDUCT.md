# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Mega Vault Protect welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Everyone should be able to contribute, ask questions, and discuss ideas without harassment or discrimination.

This commitment applies regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, appearance, religion, sexual orientation, or other personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

When participating in this project:

- Treat others with respect and patience.
- Give constructive feedback about the work.
- Respect different perspectives and experience levels.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them.
- Respect personal boundaries and requests to stop an interaction.
- Protect other people's privacy and confidential information.

Technical disagreements are welcome. Explain your reasoning, provide evidence, and criticize ideas without attacking people.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks, insults, or deliberately humiliating others.
- Sexual harassment or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Sharing private vault contents, passwords, or other secrets without authorization.
- Repeated unwanted contact or continuing disruptive behavior after being asked to stop.
- Retaliation against someone for reporting a concern or participating in an investigation.

## Scope

This code applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other community spaces managed by the project.

It also applies when someone represents the project publicly, including through an official account or at an event.

## Reporting concerns

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer:

**[REPLACE WITH A PRIVATE REPORTING EMAIL BEFORE PUBLISHING]**

Include whatever information you feel comfortable sharing, such as where the incident occurred, what happened, and relevant links or messages. Screenshots are optional. You do not need to disclose personal characteristics or confront the person involved before reporting.

Do not post sensitive reports in public issues. Security vulnerabilities should follow the security-reporting guidance in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

If the concern involves the person receiving reports, you may use the hosting platform's reporting tools. For incidents on GitHub, use GitHub's abuse-reporting process.

## How reports are handled

The maintainer will review reports as availability allows, gather relevant context, and decide on an appropriate response. There is no guaranteed response deadline.

Reports will be handled as privately as reasonably possible. Information may need to be shared with people involved in resolving the incident or with the hosting platform. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

Responses may include:

- A request to change behavior.
- Removal or editing of inappropriate content.
- A warning or temporary participation restriction.
- A permanent ban from project spaces.

The response will depend on the severity, impact, and pattern of behavior. Serious incidents may result in immediate restrictions without a prior warning.

Anyone handling a report must disclose conflicts of interest and step aside when a suitable alternative reviewer is available. Reports made in good faith must not lead to retaliation, even when the available evidence does not establish a violation.

## Maintainer responsibility

Project maintainers are responsible for applying this code fairly, explaining moderation decisions when appropriate, and keeping the reporting contact current.

Maintainers are subject to the same expectations as other participants. This document should be reviewed when project ownership or community reporting arrangements change.
